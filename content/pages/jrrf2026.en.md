Fraxinus Pj will exhibit at Japan RepRap Festival 2026 (JRRF2026), held on May 30-31, 2026 at Tokyo Ryutsu Center 2nd Exhibition Hall E. Our booth is **M-208**, and MakerChip exchange is available.

The booth will focus on actual compact, high-speed CoreXY 3D printers from the Fraxinus community, including related builds, modifications, and derivative machines. Visitors can see real machines and talk directly with builders about design and assembly.

## Planned Exhibits

Fraxinus is a digital fabrication machine project built around compact machines that remain fast for their print volume. At JRRF2026, the booth will introduce actual Fraxinus machines, derivative builds, and related work from community members.
