There are 9 types of compact CoreXY micro 3D printers using acrylic frames, suitable for on-the-go demos and feature introductions. Community-made derivatives are also listed on the [community page](/en/docs/community/).

In addition, there are also [printers designed and built by community members](../community).

|-------|---------------|------------|-------|-------------|
| 000w4 | 95×117×95 | 50 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (external) | Smallest in the series, touch screen |
| 00 | 160×157×194 | 70 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (can be internal) | Original model |
| 00w | 153×152×176 | 70 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (internal) | Fully self-contained with built-in battery |
| 00w2 | 147×152×162 | 66 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (external) | Touch screen, more compact |
| 00w3 | 147×160×147 | 66 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (external) | Further height reduction, redesigned gantry |
| 00w3 (mold) | 147×160×147 | 66 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (external) | 3D-printed version of the 00w3 |
| 00w4 | 125×137×125 | 80 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (external) | Largest print area in the 00 series, touch screen |
| 0w | 203×215×203 | 120 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (can be internal) | Larger print volume |
| 0w3 | 197×215×198 | 116 mm cube | USB-PD / mobile battery (external) | Larger print volume derived from the 00w3 |

## Fraxinus000w4

A compact CoreXY 3D printer with a 50 mm square print area that runs on USB-PD power, making it well suited to on-the-go introductions. With a body size of 95×117×95 mm (excluding protruding parts), it is the smallest model in the series. It can also run on an external mobile battery and has a touch screen on the front. ([Announcement post on X](https://x.com/fukumay1/status/2069808983955964148))

## Fraxinus00

The original model in the series. It has a 70 mm cube print volume and runs on USB-PD power or an external mobile battery.

![Fraxinus00 render image](/images/Fraxinus00.jpg)

## Fraxinus00w

This model can house a mobile battery internally, making it fully self-contained for on-the-go use. The tradeoff is a slightly larger body size, but the print volume remains the same 70 mm cube as the 00.

![Fraxinus00w render image](/images/Fraxinus00w.jpg)

![Fraxinus00w real build photo](/images/Fraxinus00w-photo-1.jpg)

## Fraxinus00w2

By removing the built-in battery and relying on an external USB-PD power supply, the body is even more compact. It also features a touch screen for easier operation. The print size is slightly smaller at 66 mm cube.

![Fraxinus00w2 render image](/images/Fraxinus00w2.jpg)

![Fraxinus00w2 real build photo](/images/Fraxinus00w2-photo-1.jpg)

![Fraxinus00w2 comparison image](/images/Fraxinus00w2-compare.jpg)

## Fraxinus00w3

Keeps the same 66 mm cube print size as the 00w2 while further reducing the body height (162 mm to 147 mm). A redesigned gantry structure achieves an even more compact form factor.

![Fraxinus00w3 render image 1](/images/Fraxinus00w3-1.jpg)

![Fraxinus00w3 real build photo](/images/Fraxinus00w3-photo-1.jpg)

## Fraxinus00w3 (mold)

Keeps the same 66 mm cube print size as the 00w2 while further reducing the body height (162 mm to 147 mm). A redesigned gantry structure achieves an even more compact form factor. This is the 3D-printed version of the 00w3.

## Fraxinus00w4

A compact CoreXY 3D printer with an 80 mm square print area that runs on USB-PD power, making it well suited to on-the-go introductions. With a body size of 125×137×125 mm (excluding protruding parts), it has the largest print area in the 00 series. It can also run on an external mobile battery and has a touch screen on the front.

![Rear three-quarter render of the Fraxinus00w4 with spec text showing an 80x80x80 mm print size and 125x137x125 mm body size](/images/Fraxinus00w4-01.jpg)

![Front render of the Fraxinus00w4 showing its front touch screen](/images/Fraxinus00w4-02.jpg)

## Fraxinus0w

The largest micro printer in the series with a 120 mm cube print volume. It offers the same build area as a VORON v0.2 in a slightly smaller body.

![Fraxinus0w rev1 render image](/images/Fraxinus0w-rev1.jpg)

![Fraxinus0w rev2 render image](/images/Fraxinus0w-rev2.jpg)

## Fraxinus0w3

A derivative of the 00w3 with a 116 mm cube print volume, making it one of the largest micro printers in the series. It offers nearly the same build area as a VORON v0.2 in a slightly smaller body.
